# Organic search rollout

The homepage links to `/buying-guides`, which links to three permanent categories
and model price guides. `/sitemap.xml` contains these canonical URLs. `/robots.txt`
advertises the sitemap. All content and navigation are rendered on the server.

Model guides require five distinct GBP sold listings sold and collected within
90 days, explicitly used/refurbished and classified as ordinary working condition.
Duplicate listing IDs are counted once. The hub highlights the five models with
the largest samples and links to every other eligible model. No evidence is
invented and these routes do not schedule external research. Price summaries
are unadjusted listing-level statistics across specifications, with that limitation
explained alongside the evidence. A guide without enough evidence returns 404.

After deployment, the site owner must verify the domain in Google Search Console,
submit `https://laptoplander.com/sitemap.xml`, and inspect the homepage, hub and
one category/model URL. Account access is not available through the repository.
Check indexed pages, non-brand impressions, clicks and outbound deal clicks over
the following weeks. Search phrases are initial hypotheses, not verified volumes.

Verify the public reverse proxy passes the new paths through to the application.
The app cannot establish Search Console ownership or verify public indexing itself.
